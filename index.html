The Secret? The 4 Laws of Behavior Change

Based on James Clear's Atomic Habits—the most powerful behavior change system ever created.

👁️

Make It Invisible

Your child can't crave what they can't see. We remove ALL visual cues. Devices disappear. Apps get buried.

Result: Within 3-5 days, "Can I have the tablet?" requests reduce by 70%.

✨

Make Alternatives Attractive

We make OTHER activities irresistibly appealing. Through design (not force), alternatives become MORE attractive than screens.

Result: Your child starts CHOOSING to build Lego, play outside, or read.

🚧

Make It Difficult

We add strategic friction. Passwords. Physical barriers. Time delays. Each barrier gives the brain a chance to resist.

Result: Impulsive use drops 85%.

🎯

Make It Unrewarding

We remove satisfaction from screen use while creating immediate rewards for screen-free behavior.

Result: Neural pathways rewire. New habits form.
