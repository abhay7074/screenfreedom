Screen Freedom sells digital products (eBooks, PDFs, guides, templates) that are delivered instantly via email. Unlike physical products, digital products cannot be "returned" once accessed. Here's why refunds are not possible:

1.1 Instant Access

  • Once downloaded, we cannot "take back" the product

1.2 No Way to Verify Returns

1.3 Industry Standard

  • No refunds on digital products is the standard practice across the industry
  • Platforms like Amazon Kindle, Udemy, and most digital product sellers have similar policies
  • This protects creators from fraud and abuse
