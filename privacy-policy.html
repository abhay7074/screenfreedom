At Screen Freedom, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and purchase our digital products.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you make a purchase or interact with our website, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number
  • Payment Information: Payment details are processed securely through our third-party payment processors (Razorpay, Instamojo, or similar). We do not store your credit card or debit card information on our servers.
  • Order Information: Purchase history, transaction details, download records

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information:

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device type
  • Usage Data: Pages viewed, time spent on pages, click patterns, referring URLs
  • Cookies: We use cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Cookie Policy section)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

2.1 To Process Transactions

  • Complete your purchase and deliver digital products
  • Send order confirmations and download links
  • Process refunds (when applicable per our Refund Policy)

2.2 To Communicate With You

  • Respond to your inquiries and customer support requests
  • Send important updates about your purchase
  • Provide product updates and improvements
  • Send marketing communications (only with your consent - you can opt-out anytime)

2.3 To Improve Our Services

  • Analyze website usage and user behavior
  • Improve our website functionality and user experience
  • Develop new features and products
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

2.4 For Marketing and Advertising

  • Display targeted advertisements (Facebook Ads, Google Ads)
  • Retarget website visitors
  • Measure advertising effectiveness

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. However, we may share your information with:

3.1 Service Providers

  • Payment Processors: Razorpay, Instamojo, PayPal (to process payments)
  • Email Service Providers: To send transactional and marketing emails
  • Analytics Services: Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel (to analyze website traffic)
  • Hosting Providers: To store and deliver our digital products

3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government regulation, or to:

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect our rights, property, or safety
  • Prevent fraud or abuse
  • Respond to emergency situations

3.3 Business Transfers

If Screen Freedom is acquired, merged, or sold, your information may be transferred to the new owner as part of the business assets.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

4.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us recognize you, remember your preferences, and improve your experience.

4.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function (e.g., shopping cart, login sessions)
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our website (Google Analytics)
  • Marketing Cookies: Used for targeted advertising (Facebook Pixel, Google Ads)
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences

4.3 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality. To opt-out of targeted advertising:

  • Google Ads: Visit Google Ad Settings
  • Facebook Ads: Visit Facebook Ad Preferences

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • SSL Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using SSL/TLS
  • Secure Payment Processing: Payment information is processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways
  • Access Controls: Only authorized personnel have access to personal data
  • Regular Security Audits: We regularly review and update our security practices

Note: While we take reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide you with the products and services you purchased
  • Comply with legal obligations (tax records, financial records)
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

Retention Periods:

  • Transaction Data: 7 years (Indian tax law requirement)
  • Marketing Data: Until you unsubscribe or request deletion
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized (no personal identifiers)

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

7.1 Right to Access

You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

7.2 Right to Correction

You can request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.

7.3 Right to Deletion

You can request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.

7.4 Right to Opt-Out

You can opt-out of marketing communications at any time by:

  • Clicking the "unsubscribe" link in our emails
  • Contacting us directly (see Contact section)

7.5 Right to Data Portability

You can request a copy of your data in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@screenfreedom.com

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and products are intended for parents and guardians of children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 years of age.

If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., payment processors, social media). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. Please review their privacy policies before providing any information.

10. International Users

Screen Freedom is based in India. If you are accessing our website from outside India, please note that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in India.

By using our website, you consent to the transfer of your information to India, which may have different data protection laws than your country.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy on our website

Your continued use of our website after changes constitute acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

12. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to analyze website traffic and user behavior. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous information such as:

  • Pages viewed and time spent on pages

This data is aggregated and anonymized. Google Analytics does not collect personally identifiable information.

To opt-out: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on

To opt-out: Visit Facebook Ad Preferences
